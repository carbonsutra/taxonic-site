# Spreadsheets

Taxonic helps process and classify structured data efficiently.

## Spreadsheet Classification

Use spreadsheet-based workflows to organize large collections of records and prepare data for classification.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Prepare your spreadsheet with title and description columns.
2. Identify the classification standard you want to use.
3. Submit records for classification through your integration.
4. Review the generated classification suggestions.
5. Export or store the processed results.

## Benefits

- Consistent classification across records.
- Reduced manual categorization.
- Improved metadata organization.
- Efficient processing of large datasets.

For automated classification, integrate your spreadsheet workflow with the Taxonic API.
